# Product: Flanders Wood Floors & Construction LLC website

## Platform
- Platform: web
- Stack: static single-file HTML/CSS/JS (`index.html`), hosted on GitHub Pages. Only external resource: Cal Sans from Google Fonts.

## Users
- **Primary:** homeowners in and around Flanders, NJ (northern NJ, 973 area code) planning a flooring project (hardwood, LVP, tile) or a kitchen, bathroom or deck job. They usually arrive from a Facebook group post, on a phone, inside Facebook's in-app browser.
- **Secondary:** the owner of the business, who sees the site first and decides whether to keep it.

## Purpose
Give the business a real home on the web that turns their "free estimate" ads into estimate requests: who they are, what they install, where, and one easy way to ask for a free estimate (a pre-written text) or call.

## Positioning
A local flooring installer that also does kitchens, bathrooms and decks, offering free estimates. Casual, friendly, neighbourly tone ("Have a great night" on their posts).

## Capabilities
- Tap-to-call phone number (973-252-9051) in the header.
- Free-estimate form that composes a text message to the business (job type, town, size, timing, notes), with a call/copy fallback.
- A floating text button after the hero.
- Facebook link to see more of their work.
